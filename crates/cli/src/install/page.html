    Where is your domain's DNS?
    forkrail sets up your apps' addresses and HTTPS certificates by itself through Cloudflare's DNS, which is free. Your domain stays with the company you bought it from; only its DNS moves:
    1. Add your domain to Cloudflare (free plan).
    2. Where you bought the domain (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Route 53, …), replace its nameservers with the two Cloudflare shows you.
    3. When Cloudflare says the domain is active (usually within an hour), pick Cloudflare here.

    Your apps get name.example.com, and your dashboard dashboard.example.com.

    Where your apps run. Pick the one closest to your visitors.

    forkrail is installed here already, so its domain and region stay as they are.

    Cloudflare API token (optional) Create the token on Cloudflare ↗
    1. Everything is filled in: click Continue to summary, then Create Token.
    2. Copy the token, and paste it here.

    The token from the earlier run is saved. Paste a new one only to replace it.

    To keep it to this one domain, pick the domain under Zone Resources before you continue.

    For spending and certificate alerts. AWS sends one email to confirm it. Without one, the spending limit still stops your apps at the limit, but no one is told.
